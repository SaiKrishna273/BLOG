Mahendra Singh Dhoni – Captain Cool & Icon

About MS Dhoni

Mahendra Singh Dhoni is a former Indian international cricketer and one of the most successful captains in Indian cricket history. Known for his calm personality, exceptional wicketkeeping skills, powerful finishing ability, and outstanding leadership, he is popularly known as "Captain Cool."

Born in Ranchi, Jharkhand, Dhoni rose through domestic cricket before making his international debut for India. Under his captaincy, India won the 2007 ICC T20 World Cup, the 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup, and the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy.

Career Milestones

2004: International Debut

Made his ODI debut for India against Bangladesh in Chittagong in December 2004.

2007: T20 World Cup Triumph

Led India to victory in the inaugural ICC T20 World Cup in South Africa.

2011: World Cup Triumph

Captained India to victory in the ICC Cricket World Cup 2011 and finished the final with an iconic six.

2013: Champions Trophy Victory

Led India to victory in the ICC Champions Trophy 2013.
